Construction equations

For strand j = 0, …, s − 1 and height h ∈ [0, 2π], set θ = (rh + 2πj)/s. The open strand follows (x, y, h), with x = a cos θ and y = (b/ℓ) sin(ℓθ). Closure maps it to ((R + x) cos h, (R + x) sin h, y). Here a = b = 1 and R = 2, leaving a hole through the center of the closed braid.
